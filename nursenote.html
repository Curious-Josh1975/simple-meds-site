Installing NurseNote

NurseNote is an Android app you download straight from jslegends.com, not from Google Play. That means your phone will ask a couple of extra questions the first time.

  1. Open the downloaded file. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser or Files app. This is called Install unknown apps. Turn it on for that app.
  2. You may see a warning, possibly from Play Protect. That's normal for an app that doesn't come from the Play Store. Tap through to install.
  3. Open NurseNote and allow the microphone when it asks.

Android phones only. NurseNote doesn't work on iPhone.
